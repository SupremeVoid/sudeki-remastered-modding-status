Sudeki Remastered: modding status

Last updated 2026-10-28

What mods can change in Sudeki Remastered today, what is experimental and what is still planned. Mods go into the mods folder (or the configuration tool's Mods tab) and never touch the installed game. We update this page as the work moves on.

Mods in general

Mod folders and .zip files, mod.ini (name, author, version, description in five languages)Supported
Load order, conflicts between mods, switching mods on and offSupportedIn the configuration tool's Mods tab.
TexMod / uMod packages (.tpf) turned into modsSupportedAll 406 known texture hashes matched.
Checking a mod (errors and warnings for every file)SupportedModding Suite, Mods tab.
Changes while the game runsSupportedPictures, .materials and .lights are taken again when saved ([Mods] HotReload). Everything else applies at start.
Requires= (a mod that needs another one)Supported, with limitsChecked and warned about; the game does not act on it yet.

Textures and models

Replacing any file of the game's archives (models, textures, entities, tables)SupportedSame format as the original.
High-resolution textures (.png, .jpg, .dds BC1-BC3, BC7)SupportedAny resolution; animated textures frame by frame.
New textures under new namesSupportedFor a mod's model that names them.
Surface relief and parallax per texture (.materials)Supported
Models exported to glTFSupportedAll 2,432 models of the game.
Models imported from glTF (props, effects, outfits)Supported, with limitsCharacters keep their skeleton (same bones). Not yet: tangent frames, two-component UVs, cutscene morphs, interface models.
Showing the name of the texture under the cursor in the gamePlanned

Sound and video

Movies (Bink 1 .bik)Supported
Whole sound banks (.xwb, .xsb, .sf)Supported
Single sounds (.wav)SupportedReplaces one entry of a wave bank.
New sounds and cues, retimed speechExperimentalLooping new cues and the lip sync of retimed speech are not tested yet.

Texts, data and balancing

Texts (.strings), in every languageSupportedSeveral mods add up.
Data values (.patch)Supported, with limitsSeveral mods add up. Not yet for character templates (enemy HP, XP, skills): the mod check refuses them, and a patched enemy is not tested in the game yet.
A balancing editor in the Modding Suite (enemies, skills, the XP table)Planned
Difficulty scaling (damage, enemy HP, XP, money)PlannedOn top of whatever balancing mods set.
Interfaces (menus, the HUD)PlannedResearch first: where the layouts live and what a mod can replace.

Animation

Poses and animations for photo mode (.anim)SupportedMade with the Animation Editor.
Replacing the game's own animation clips (walks, idles, attacks)SupportedOn any skeleton: party, NPCs, monsters.
Clip events (footsteps, sounds, effects)Supported
New clips played by a character's statesSupportedThrough a .patch of the character's .ani file.

Light and look

New lights in zones, changes to the game's lamps, torches and fires (.lights)SupportedPlaced in the Modding Suite's zone view; ray-traced shadows where ray tracing is on.
Colour grading (LUTs) from modsPlanned

World and content

New doors, rooms and places to sit (.doors, .rooms, .places)ExperimentalA new door into a tavern and places to sit work in the game. A copied room loads but its geometry is not drawn yet.
New characters (NPCs with an outfit, a name, lines, a walk)ExperimentalMade in the Modding Suite. Voices for new lines are not supported yet.
Script mods (quests, events, mechanics)In progressA disassembler, an assembler and a linker exist; a decompiler and a compiler are next.
Whole new levelsNot plannedNeeds collision and navigation formats that are not decoded.
Save games, the game's codeNot plannedMods change the game's data, not its program or your saves.

Tools for modders

Configuration tool, Mods tabInstall, order, switch and import mods; the modding guide in five languages.
Sudeki Modding SuiteEvery asset of the game read from your own install: textures, models, characters, animations, zones, sounds, texts, data. Checks mods and makes them (materials, lights, new characters, glTF import).
Sudeki Animation EditorPoses and animations for Tal, Ailish, Buki and Elco, and edits of the game's own clips for any skeleton.
Extract Game AssetsThe game's models (glTF) and textures (PNG) from your own install, as a start for mods.
Sudeki Remastered is a fan project; you need your own copy of Sudeki. Source of this page: github.com/SupremeVoid/sudeki-remastered-modding-status
